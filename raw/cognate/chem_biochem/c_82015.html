Faculty of Science  Chemistry and Biochemistry  CH271 

CH271
Chemistry and Sustainability
0.5 Credit

Hours per week:
  • Lecture/Discussion: 3
  • Lab: 1.5 (biweekly)

This course covers fundamentals of general chemistry and may include topics such as stoichiometry, atomic structure, bonding and molecular structure, gases, liquids, solids, and solutions, electrochemistry, kinetics and thermodynamics, equilibrium, and acids and bases. Applications of these concepts in understanding and working toward solutions for current sustainability challenges including pollution, climate change, and clean water and energy are highlighted throughout the course.

Additional Course Information
Prerequisites
Registration Status: Software Engineering program
