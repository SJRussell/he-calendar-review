Faculty of Science  Kinesiology and Physical Education  KP381 

KP381
Advanced Psychology of Sport and Exercise
0.5 Credit - Fall/Winter

Hours per week:
  • Lecture/Discussion: 3

This is an advanced course exploring theoretical and applied aspects of psychological concepts across both sport and exercise domains.

Additional Course Information
Prerequisites
KP181; registration status: Year 3 of BKin program
Exclusions
KP410, KP412, KP414.
