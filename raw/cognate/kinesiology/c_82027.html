Faculty of Science  Kinesiology and Physical Education  KP495 

KP495
Strength and Conditioning Practicum
0.5 Credit

Hours per week:
  • Lecture/Discussion: 3

This advanced practicum provides senior Kinesiology students with hands-on experience under the supervision of a Certified Strength and Conditioning Coach.  Students will complete the practicum hours in an applied sport performance environment and participate in weekly group learning sessions to integrate theory with professional practice. Practicum hours may run within and outside of regular classroom hours.

Through direct involvement with the Laurier Golden Hawks Strength and Conditioning Unit, students will gain exposure to all aspects of athlete development, including coaching team training sessions, leading warm-ups, evaluating movement, performing fitness and performance assessments, assisting with return-to-play programming, and engaging in professional communication within the Athletics community.

The practicum emphasizes the development of practical coaching skills, professional conduct, and the ability to apply exercise science principles in real-world sport and performance contexts.

Additional Course Information
Prerequisites
KP322; Permission of department in consultation with Laurier Golden Hawks Strength and Conditioning Unit; and Registration status: Year 4 BKin program
Co-requisites
KP426
Notes
Must have 1 year of prior successful participation in the Laurier Golden Hawks Strength and Conditioning Unit. This practicum continues this participation in your 4th year.
