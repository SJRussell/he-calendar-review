Faculty of Science  Health Sciences  HE604B 

HE604B
Applied Methods in Molecular and Medical Sciences
0.5 Credit

This course is designed to introduce students to molecular medical sciences (MMS) research and to help develop research and analytical skills. Students actively engage with the theory and logic of MMS research, exploring the ethical principles guiding the research process, and learning about a variety of research methodologies commonly used in the MMS field. Topics include experimental design, data collection and analysis of results, and how to present and communicate scientific information in both oral and written formats. Research excellence through diversity is explored to demonstrate the importance of equity, diversity, and inclusion best practices in basic science research. The emphasis of this course is to provide students with practical knowledge of the research process and prepare them for conducting their own research. At the end, students have a better understanding of how to evaluate both their own research and that of others, and how to strategically and logically develop a research plan.
